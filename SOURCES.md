# Sources for the 21 November 2026 news image

Compiled on Saturday 21 November 2026, about 11:00 Asia/Shanghai (UTC+8).
The poster only uses original typography and geometry. No photographs and no
image-generation models were used. Event dates are printed on each card so a
Friday or Thursday development is not presented as if it happened on Saturday.

Xinhua's 21 November summary listed a Dragon splashdown time of "approximately
4:55 p.m. Pacific Time." NASA's own blog, written after undocking, says
approximately 4:55 p.m. EST (1:55 p.m. PST). The image follows NASA.

## 2. EU–UK summit agreements

- Claim: Leaders met in Brussels on 20 November 2026 and welcomed concluded
  negotiations on (a) a Common Sanitary and Phytosanitary Area, aimed at
  applying from 1 March 2028 so that the vast majority of animal, plant and
  related movements between Great Britain (and the Crown Dependencies) and
  the EU would take place without the certificates or controls now required;
  (b) linking the EU and UK emissions trading systems, aimed from 2028, as a
  basis for mutual recognition of allowances and possible CBAM exemptions;
  (c) a Youth Experience Scheme for 100,000 young people from the EU and from
  the UK respectively, to reside for up to three years, with the agreement
  aimed no later than 2028 and opportunities no later than January 2030.
- Official source: UK Government, "UK-EU Summit 2026 Joint Statement (HTML),"
  updated 20 November 2026, paragraphs 39, 48 and 49.
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ukeu-summit-2026-key-documents/uk-eu-summit-2026-joint-statement-html
- Official source: European Commission, Directorate-General for Climate Action,
  "Negotiations finalised to link EU and UK Emissions Trading Systems,"
  20 November 2026. The Commission text says the parties have committed to
  applying the ETS link as of 1 January 2028, subject to ratification.
  https://climate.ec.europa.eu/news-other-reads/news/negotiations-finalised-link-eu-and-uk-emissions-trading-systems-2026-11-20_en
- Also carried in Xinhua's 21 November world summary (see item 3).

## 4. ICJ judgment on Myanmar and the Rohingya

- Claim: On Thursday 19 November 2026 the International Court of Justice, in
  The Gambia v. Myanmar, found Myanmar responsible for genocide against the
  Rohingya during the 2017 Rakhine "clearance operations." UN News describes
  this as the first such ruling against a State. The judgment is final and
  binding. The Court ordered Myanmar to investigate and, where warranted,
  prosecute; to allow safe, voluntary and dignified return; and to report
  within two years on restitution and compensation.
- Source: UN News, "World’s top court finds Myanmar guilty of genocide against
  Rohingya," 19 November 2026.
  https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168836
- The official unofficial press release (No. 2026/30, The Hague,
  19 November 2026) is archived at:
  https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/178-20261119-pre-01-00-en.pdf
- Saturday circulation: the SBS 21 November 2026 morning bulletin also led
  with community reaction to the ruling (same SBS bulletin URL as item 1).

The image labels this card "19 NOV, STILL IN SATURDAY BRIEFS" so it is not
presented as a 21 November courtroom event.

## 5. SpaceX Dragon return (scheduled on Saturday)

- Claim: The uncrewed SpaceX Dragon undocked from the space-facing port of
  the ISS Harmony module at 12:05 p.m. EST on Friday 20 November 2026, after
  a command from SpaceX ground controllers. NASA said it is due to splash
  down off the California coast at approximately 4:55 p.m. EST
  (1:55 p.m. PST) on Saturday 21 November 2026. NASA will not livestream
  the splashdown and said it would post updates on its space-station blog.
- Source: NASA ISS blog, Mark A. Garcia, "Dragon Undocks from Station Packed
  With Science and Cargo," 20 November 2026, 12:08 p.m.
  https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/general-blog/2026/11/20/dragon-undocks-from-station-packed-with-science-and-cargo/
- Same timing in the earlier NASA post "Dragon Prepares to Depart Station for
  Earth Return," 20 November 2026, 11:47 a.m.:
  https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/general-blog/2026/11/20/dragon-prepares-to-depart-station-for-earth-return/

As of compilation on Saturday morning 21 November 2026 in Asia/Shanghai
(still Friday evening in the US Eastern zone), splashdown had not yet
occurred. The image therefore says "scheduled, not confirmed" and does not
claim a completed landing.
